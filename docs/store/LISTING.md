## App name

Add Call

## Short description

Voice and video calls and chat with the people you add. No phone number needed.

## Full description

Add Call is a simple way to call and chat with the people you know: voice
calls, video calls and messages, over Wi-Fi or mobile data.

RINGS LIKE A PHONE CALL
Calls ring your phone even when Add Call is closed, full screen on a locked
phone, with Answer and Decline. Missed calls and new messages show as
notifications.

CALLS THAT JUST WORK
• Voice and video calls, with mute, camera on or off, and front or back camera
• Calls connect on mobile networks and strict Wi-Fi
• See when someone is online or already on a call
• Your call history in Calls, with one tap to call back

CHAT
• Text, photos, files up to 25 MB, voice messages and stickers
• Sent, delivered and read ticks, and "typing…"
• Delete a message for everyone within 2 days

FIND PEOPLE BY @USERNAME
Everyone picks a unique @username. Find someone by their exact @username (or
email address) and add them to your list. There's no public directory of
users to browse.

PRIVACY AND SAFETY
• Sign in with a one-time code sent to your email: no passwords to remember
• Block anyone: they can't call or message you
• Report people or messages to our team
• Delete your account and everything in it from Settings at any time
• No ads, and no tracking or analytics
• Calls are encrypted in transit and never recorded

Add Call also works in your web browser at call.adddots.com, with the same
account.
